## Mesmer

- Concerning damage weapons:
  - Usually it is two of the three options: Dagger/Sword, Spear and Greatsword.
  - Sword/Focus is good for pulling enemies and for reflects, but deals less damage.
- Concerning traits:
  - The default damage trait lines are Domination, Dueling and the elite specialization. For open world, Illusions is an amazing alternative since it provides you with boons you might not have. If you want to take the Illusions trait line, I would replace Domination, but only if you are not using a greatsword. If you want to run a greatsword, I would just not take the Illusions trait line, because you want Bountiful Blades, and Dueling just gives so many damage buffs.
  - If you use a greatsword, always choose Bountiful Blades over Empowered Illusions.
  - If you need sustain you can go with Inspiration. If you use a greatsword replace Dueling, otherwise replace Domination. Illusions + Inspiration is also possible, but you sacrifice a lot of damage here. The Inspiration traits would be: Sympathetic Visage, Ego Restoration, Restorative Illusions.
  - The Inspiration major adept (the first choosable trait) is quite flexible: you can take Medic's Feedback to revive allies, Restorative Mantras to heal allies with mantras and Sympathetic Visage so phantasms take conditions from you, which is the most "solo" option.

## Thief

- Concerning traits: Deadly Arts and Critical Strikes are the go-to damage options. Trickery is an open-world option which gives you more initiative and boons for your allies.

### Open World (Event/Tagging) Power Daredevil

- Weapons: Shortbow + Staff
- Traits: Trickery gives you boons and more initiative, Deadly Arts gives you damage, Acrobatics improves your dodges and Critical Strikes gives you damage. Choose two of the four to complement Daredevil.
  - Trickery: Burst of Agility, Bountiful Theft, Quick Pockets
  - Deadly Arts: Dagger Training (or Mug for sustain), Revealed Training, Executioner
  - Acrobatics: Pumping Up, Swindler's Equilibrium, Upper Hand
  - Critical Strikes: Twin Fangs, Practiced Tolerance, No Quarter (or Invigorating Precision for sustain)
  - Daredevil: Brawler's Tenacity (or Marauder's Resilience if you go melee), Havoc Specialist (or Staff Master if you use a staff), Lotus Training for tagging (or Bounding Dodger for damage)
- Utility skills:
  - Channeled Vigor
  - Signet of Agility
  - Haste or Assassin's Signet
  - Shadowstep
  - (You can also use Fist Flurry for melee damage or Impairing Daggers for ranged damage)
  - Dagger Storm (or Thieves Guild for more damage on a higher cooldown)
- Relics: Thief, Zakiros
- Sigils: don't forget Fire and Stamina on the Shortbow and Strength on the Staff. Sigil of Blood is also interesting for life steal.

### Open World Power Deadeye
