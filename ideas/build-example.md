# Builds

One build per `##` heading. Copy the block below for every build and fill it
in. Names are matched ignoring case and punctuation, so `wash the pain away`
works as well as `"Wash the Pain Away!"`. A trait may also be given as
`top`, `middle` or `bottom` instead of its name. Lines marked *optional* can
be left out.

The gear comes from the optimizer link, so nothing about stats, runes, sigils,
relic, food or infusions needs writing here. Only what the optimizer cannot
know goes under "Gear refinements": skins, the underwater weapon, a piece you
deliberately deviate on.
