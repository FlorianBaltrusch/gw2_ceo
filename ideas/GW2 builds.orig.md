## Mesmer

- Concerning damage weapons: 
  
  - Usually it is two of the three options:Dagger/Sword + Spear + Greatsword
  
  - Sword/Focus is good for pulling enemies and reflects, but less damage.

- Concerning traits: 
  
  - The default damage trait lines are Domination, Dueling and the elite specilisation. For Open World, Illusions is an amazing alternative sicne it provides you with boons you might not have. If you want to take the Illusion trait line, I would replace Domination, but only if you are not using a Greatsword. If you want to run a Greatsword, I would just not take the Illusion trait line, because you want Bountiful Blade and Dueling just gives so many damage buffs.
  
  - If you use a greatsword always choose Bountiful blades over Empowered Illusions.
  
  - If you need sustain you can go with Inspiration. If you use a Greatsword replace Dueling, otherwise replace Domination. Illusion + Inspiration is also possible but you sacrifice alot of damage here. The Inspiration traits would be:
    
    - Sympathetic Visage, Ego Restoration, Restorative Illusions
  
  - The Inspiration Major Adept (the first chooseble trait) is quite flexible, you cn take Medic's Feedback to revive allies, Restorative Mantras to heal allies with Mantras and Sypathic Visages for Phantasms to take conditions from you, which is the most "solo" option.

## Thief

- Concerning Traits: Deadly Arts and Critical Strikes are the go-to damage options. Trickery is an Open World option which gives you more initiative and boons to your allies.

- Weapons: Shortbow + Staff

- Traits: Tricker gives you boons and more initiative, Deadly Arts gives you damage, Acrobatics improves your dodges and Critical Strikes gives you damage. Choose two of the four to complement Daredevil.

    - Burst of Agility, Bountiful Theft, Quick Pockets
  - Deadly Arts:
    - Dagger Training (or Mug for sustain), Reveal Training, Executioner
  - Acrobatics:
    - Pumping Up, Swindler's Equilibrium, Upper Hand

    - Twin Fangs, Practiced Tolerance, No Quarter (or Invigorating Precision for sustain)

    - Brawler's Tenacity (or Marauder's Resiliance if you go meele), Havoc Specialist (or Staff Master if you use a staff), Lotus Training for Tagging (or Bounding Dodger for damage)
